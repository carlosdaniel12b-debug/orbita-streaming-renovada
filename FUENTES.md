## Fondo espacial

Archivo: `assets/space.webp`. Creado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes; optimizado a WebP para esta página.

Prompt utilizado:

> Create a premium cinematic website hero background, landscape 1536x1024. Outer space midnight navy almost black, a breathtaking realistic blue planet with delicate atmospheric rim and sweeping thin pale silver orbital rings tilted diagonally. Planet positioned on far right around 72% x 55%, taking about 48% of composition, left 48% extremely dark quiet negative space for white headline overlay. Subtle coral sunlight illuminates the bottom right of the planet and small distant stars; elegant NASA photography aesthetic, richly detailed cloud surface, deep dimensionality, editorial streaming brand, sophisticated minimal composition. No text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no spacecraft, not a neon illustration.
